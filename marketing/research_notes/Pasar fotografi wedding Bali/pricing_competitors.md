# Bali wedding / pre-wedding / elopement photo & video: price map and competitors

Research date: 2026-10-11. Method: web search only. Direct page fetches failed with DNS errors in this environment, so every figure below comes from **search-result snippets** (vendor pages, Bridestory, blogs). The snippets were not checked against the full pages. Dates are given where the snippet or page showed one. Conversions use about IDR 16,000 = USD 1, an assumption that matches Villo Studio's own figures (IDR 4M ≈ $250, IDR 16M ≈ $980).

## (a) IDR packages marketed to Indonesian couples

### Takeaway
At the low and mid end, Indonesian-market wedding coverage in Bali runs about **IDR 2–5M for photo-only (4–8h)** and **IDR 4.8–12M for photo+video**. Established studios (Axioo, Lavene) charge **IDR 35M photo / 70M photo+video**. Pre-wedding starts around **IDR 1.3–2.5M** for a short 1–2 location shoot and reaches 7–8M for photo+video or premium vendors.

### Inferences
- **IDR price map** (my synthesis):
  | Tier | Wedding photo-only | Wedding photo+video | Pre-wedding |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Entry | 2–3.5M (4–6h, 1 photographer) | 4.8–6.5M (1P+1V, 8h/half day) | 1.3–2.5M (1–2h, 1–2 locations) |
  | Mid | 4–9M (full day, 1–2 photographers) | 9–12M (full day, 1–2P + 1–2V) | 2.5–7.5M (photo+video around 7.5M) |
  | Premium | 16–45M (named studios, 2 photographers) | 35–70M+ (Axioo-tier) | 8–23M (resort/foreigner-facing) |
- **Lintas Waktu:**
  - **Essential 2.5M (6h photo):** at the floor of the entry tier. More hours than the 4–5h "Basic" at Rp2–3M. About 0–30% below small local vendors, and about 40% below Villo's 4M 2h elopement price.
  - **Duo 4M (8h photo+film):** about 15–20% below the cheapest comparable 8h 1P+1V offer found (4.8M promo / 5.8M normal), and about 55% below full-day 1P+1V at 9M.
  - **Full Story 5M (incl. pre-wedding):** wedding photo+video at entry level is about 4.8–6.5M on its own, and pre-wedding about 1.3–2.5M more, so a comparable bundle costs about 6.5–9M. Full Story is roughly **25–45% below** that.
  - **Pre-wedding from 1.5M:** in line with the cheapest tier (1.3–2.5M).
  - Overall, Lintas Waktu is at or below the bottom of the IDR market. It is not far below the cheapest Denpasar vendors, but it is far below any branded studio.

### Gaps
- No 2025–26 Instagram price lists could be fetched. Many IDR pages are undated or from 2020–2024.
- Did not find out how often IDR vendors deliver same-day edits, or their delivery times.
- No benchmark found for "editorial 750K" or "personal 500K". The only general reference is that an Indonesian photographer fee starts around Rp500K ([Popbela](https://www.popbela.com/relationship/married/ingin-foto-pre-wedding-dengan-biaya-murah-cek-tipsnya-di-sini-00-nb316-s1lts3/amp)), which is not Bali-specific.

## (b) USD/AUD packages marketed to foreign couples

### Takeaway
Bali-based photographers selling to foreigners charge about **$150–700 for 2–4h coverage**, **$450–1,600 for a full day photo-only**, and **$800–2,500 for photo+video**. International or luxury photographers charge $2,500–8,000+. Marketplace couple shoots (Airbnb, Bali Photographer, Pictrip) cost **$65–460**.

### Inferences
- **USD price map:**
  | Tier | Elopement / couple 1–4h | Full-day photo | Full-day photo+video | Pre-wedding |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Entry | $65–300 (marketplaces, BPSO) | $450–750 | $800–1,000 | $100–460 |
  | Mid | $500–1,000 | $1,000–1,600 | $1,500–2,500 | $850–1,600 |
  | Premium | $1,500–6,000 | $2,200–5,000 | $2,500–8,000+ | $2,000–3,400 |
- In USD, Lintas Waktu's Essential costs about $155, Duo about $250 and Full Story about $310. That is **50–80% below** even entry foreigner-facing local vendors for comparable hours: BPSO charges $450 for 8h photo and $800+ for photo+video. Personal 500K (about $31) and editorial 750K (about $47) are below Airbnb couple shoots ($99–270).
- This suggests there is room to price foreigners separately in USD, or to raise IDR prices toward the mid tier, without leaving the market.

### Gaps
- Flytographer's Bali prices did not appear in search. Easy Weddings AU, Junebug and WeddingWire returned nothing Bali-specific.
- Most USD figures are vendors' own "from" prices; real quotes and add-ons (travel, overtime, venue vendor fees) are not visible.

## Named competitors and positioning

### Takeaway
The market splits into three groups. **Budget local / marketplace sellers** compete on price and hours (BaliLens, fotopreweddingbali.net, hargafotopreweddingbali, BPSO, Airbnb hosts). **Mid-tier Bali brands for foreigners** sell on 2-shooter teams, film and fast reels (OneThreeOneFour, Villo, Dedot, Dreams, Bali Pixtura, Agra). **Premium/editorial studios** sell on name and luxury (Axioo, Gusmank, Terralogical, Lavene, Gusde, THEPOTOMOTO).

### Inferences
- Lintas Waktu's "duo photo+film" format competes most directly with Harga Foto Prewedding Bali (4.8M), fotopreweddingbali.net (6.5–9M) and BaliLens. It also undercuts Villo and BPSO's photo+video ($800 ≈ IDR 13M).
- Competitors' common selling points are 2 shooters, fixed edit counts, short cinematic films (2–3 min) plus social reels, and next-day reels. Lintas Waktu could stand out on delivery speed and on bilingual EN/ID client galleries.

### Gaps
- Team size, film length and delivery time were not visible for most brands without fetching their pages.
- Several prices come from listicles that may be promotional (baliweddingprices.com), and Gusmank's price conflicts between sources.
