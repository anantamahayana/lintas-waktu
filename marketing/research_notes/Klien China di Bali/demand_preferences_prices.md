# Chinese (mainland) couples & travellers: photo/video demand, preferences and prices in Bali and overseas

Research date: 2026-10-11. Many Chinese pages (zhihu, sina, hunliji) could not be fetched directly (DNS blocked). Figures below come from search-result summaries of those pages, so treat them as second-hand. Much Chinese "攻略/排名" content is paid promotion (软文) from studios.

## 1. Market size & trend: 旅拍 / pre-wedding; Bali vs other destinations

### Takeaway
I found no reliable, current figure for the size of the 旅拍 market. The marriage pool is the hard driver: registrations fell to 6.106m in 2024, the lowest on record, then rebounded to 6.763m in 2025. Bali has been a top-3 overseas island for pre-wedding shoots since the 2010s. Phuket is the cheaper rival, the Maldives the premium one.

### Cited Findings
- 2024 marriage registrations: 6.106m couples, down 20.5% YoY, the largest drop since 1978. 2025: 6.763m, up 10.76%. Q1–Q3 2025: 5.152m, up 8.5%. Rules relaxed 10 May 2025 so couples can register anywhere in China. — [Jiemian](https://www.jiemian.com/article/13114048.html); [Guangming](https://m.gmw.cn/2026-02/13/content_1304343486.htm); [Yicai](https://www.yicai.com/news/103049472.html) (media reports of Ministry of Civil Affairs data; I did not check the MCA original)
- 旅拍 demand: 13.98m person-trips in 2021, with 22.6m *forecast* for 2023. 2022 marriage registrations were 6.833m. Average wedding spend was ¥253k in 2021. — [智研咨询 chyxx](https://www.chyxx.com/industry/1149394.html) (consultancy estimate, not audited)
- About 81% of newlyweds have wedding photos taken (estimate). — [中国报告网](https://www.chinabaogao.com/free/202210/614509.html)
- The wedding photography market was described as "a ¥60bn market" (2022). — [21经济网](http://www.21jingji.com/article/20220120/herald/a5c75f678bcdba75f0d56a4a902107e9.html)
- History: in 2018 Tuniu ran overseas shoots for 4,000 couples a year, with studios in the Maldives and Bali. Photo sets cost about ¥10k in the Maldives and about ¥8k in Bali. Qunar reported overseas photo-shoot orders up 140% YoY one summer (2019). — [China Daily 2018](https://www.chinadaily.com.cn/a/201806/16/WS5b24503aa310010f8f59d4ab.html); [China Daily 2019](https://global.chinadaily.com.cn/a/201909/30/WS5d9156d5a310cf3e3556e404_1.html)
- A 2026 industry guide calls Bali a "global TOP3 island 旅拍 destination". Strengths: cliffs, rice terraces, temples. Weakness: beach quality. — [Zhihu 2026 guide](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/2032940620650637108); [Zhihu Q&A](https://www.zhihu.com/question/41467065/answer/214181507)
- Phuket is seen as the cheapest of Phuket/Maldives/Bali/Jeju but has fewer themes and looser drone rules. Maldives: most resort islands reportedly don't let Chinese photographers shoot, so couples must hire local photographers at about US$500–1,000, and it is the most expensive overall. — [Zhihu Q&A](https://www.zhihu.com/question/41467065/answer/214181507) (practitioner opinion, older)
- National Day 2025: Tuniu's hot outbound list included Japan, the Maldives, Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore and Thailand. Independent travellers favoured Bali, the Maldives and Phuket. Some Maldives hotels had more than 50% Chinese guests. — [Yicai](https://www.yicai.com/news/102853996.html)
- Domestic competition: a 2026 Sanya 旅拍 guide sets tiers at ¥5–7k economy, ¥8–12k mainstream and ¥13k+ high-end. There is also a strong Dali/Sanya domestic 旅拍 brand ecosystem. — [search summary, Sanya guide]; [Sohu Dali brands 2026](https://www.sohu.com/a/990599049_122575074)

### Inferences
- The 2025 rebound in marriages, plus 2026 being a normal year, suggests demand for pre-wedding shoots in 2026–27 is stable or slightly up. The long-run trend is down (fewer marriages). That pushes studios to sell couples' anniversary, honeymoon and solo/bestie (闺蜜) travel shoots as well.
- Bali competes on scenery variety (cliff, jungle, rice field, temple), not price. Sanya and Dali are cheaper domestic substitutes.

### Gaps
- No verified 2024–2026 size for the 旅拍 market or its overseas share. Paid reports (观研天下, 前瞻) hide the numbers.
- No Ctrip/Trip.com data on 旅拍 destination ranking. No Japan/Korea/Europe share figures.

## 2. Chinese visitors to Bali, 2024–2026

### Takeaway
China sent about 537k visitors to Bali in 2025, up about 20% on 2024 and Bali's #3 market after Australia and India. In 2026 China is #2. Arrivals spike around Chinese New Year, and Jan–Jul 2026 reached 346k.

### Cited Findings
- 2025: Bali had 6,948,754 foreign arrivals (+9.72%, BPS Bali). India sent 569k and China 537k, with China up 19.83% YoY. — [Jakarta Globe](https://jakartaglobe.id/lifestyle/bali-targets-66-million-international-visitors-in-2026); [Skift/Horwath](https://dlr.skift.com/?p=5716)
- Q1 2026: China sent 157,242 (+20.22%). March 2026 dropped to 32,497 (−58.79% vs Feb), which BPS put down to Chinese New Year timing. A blog reports February 2026 at about 78.8k (+98.5%), but I could not verify it. — [Bali Discovery](https://balidiscovery.com/foreign-tourist-arrivals-increase-1-4-in-q1-2026/); [gobaligo](https://gobaligo.id/en/blog/bali-visitor-trends-2026/)
- April 2026: 44,447. H1 2026: 285,120 (+7.77%), #2 market. Jan–Jul 2026: 346,384, ahead of India's 318,948. — [Bali Discovery Apr](https://balidiscovery.com/foreign-tourist-arrivals-to-bali-decline-in-april-2026/); [Hopetrip](https://www.hopetrip.com/en-HK/news/detail-32886.html); [riserealbali](https://riserealbali.com/guides/bali-tourism-statistics/)
- Conflicting framing: some headlines say "fewer Chinese, more Americans" (monthly dips). — [Jakarta Globe](https://jakartaglobe.id/lifestyle/bali-sees-fewer-chinese-tourists-more-americans); [TTW](https://www.travelandtourworld.com/news/article/bali-tourism-enters-a-new-era-as-american-visitors-surge-while-chinese-arrivals-decline-reshaping-island-travel-patterns-in-2026/)
- Pre-COVID, China was Bali's #2 market (more than 1m in 2019, from my background knowledge; not re-verified here).

### Inferences
- Peak windows: Chinese New Year (Jan/Feb), May Day, summer (Jul–Aug) and National Day Golden Week (1–7 Oct). Bali's dry season (Apr–Oct) suits shoots, which fits May Day, summer and October.

### Gaps
- No data on the age split, or on what share of Chinese visitors are couples or honeymooners. I did not reach the BPS primary tables.

## 3. Styles & deliverables they want

### Takeaway
The standard product is a 1-day shoot with 3–4 outfits each for bride and groom, 40–50 retouched photos (精修) plus all the raw frames (全底片), and delivery in about 20–45 days. Styles are moving from posed "影楼" looks toward documentary (纪实), clean Korean (韩式), film/vintage (胶片感/法式) and 新中式. Bali shoots centre on cliffs, sunsets, swings and Nanyang (南洋) vibes.

### Cited Findings
- Bali packages in 2026 guides: mostly 3–4 outfits each, 1 shooting day, 40–50 精修. One budget package: 4 outfits each, 1 day, 45 精修 + all raw files. — [Sina 2026 guide](https://k.sina.cn/article_7879922979_1d5ae152301901ph7q.html?from=travel); [qdxw](https://wap.qdxw.com.cn/content/20588/88/69249.html)
- Keywords in Bali guide titles: 悬崖 (cliff), 日落黄 (sunset gold), 南洋 (Nanyang), 蜜月 (honeymoon), 化妆 (makeup), 防蚊 (mosquito protection), 售后 (after-sales), 避坑 (pitfall-avoidance). — [Sina](https://k.sina.com.cn/article_7906341801_1d74133a900102205w.html); [Sina](https://k.sina.cn/article_7879922979_1d5ae152301901ph7q.html?from=travel)
- Typical domestic packages: mid tier has about 3 outfits and about 30 精修. High tier has about 5 outfits and 50+ 精修. ¥5–8k packages give 30–60 精修. Total price runs about ¥4k–15k. — [hunliji 2025](https://www.hunliji.com/bai_ke/detail_82554); [hunliji](https://www.hunliji.com/bai_ke/detail_44214)
- 旅拍 example: ¥9–12k for 4 outfits, about 200 raw frames and 50–60 精修. Travel costs are extra. — [Zhihu Q&A](https://www.zhihu.com/question/446304808) (personal opinion)
- Delivery: usually 30–45 days, 20-odd days when smooth, and 2+ months in peak season. Another source says 20–35 days (1–2 days for photo selection, 10–15 for retouching). — [Sina Hangzhou](https://k.sina.cn/article_7880068208_1d5b04c7006801jhgk.html); [chinapp 2026](https://m.chinapp.net.cn/faxianpinpai/2026/0731/245795.html)
- Style trends 2025–26: documentary/real-emotion style rising. A claimed "92% of post-95s want to record a real love story" comes from an unverifiable white paper. Korean style is shifting to a natural, lifestyle feel. Film/vintage looks are back (yellow/green tones, grain; HK-style, French retro). Fusion of 新中式, Korean minimalist and French retro. — [Sohu 2026](https://www.sohu.com/a/998412087_122641797); [Zhihu 2025](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/1931286666619913854); [Sina 2026 跟拍](https://www.sina.cn/news/article/comos_nitsawi6487348.html); [hunshaz 2024](https://www.hunshaz.com/hszgl/716.html)
- Freelance Bali options on Airbnb CN: couples/honeymoon 2h ≈ US$93 for 25 edited photos; "pre-wedding" 4h US$180; 4h day shoot US$178/person, or US$208 with drone. Swing and floating-breakfast experiences are also listed. — [Airbnb 4806918](https://zh.airbnb.com/services/4806918); [Airbnb 819820](https://zh.airbnb.com/services/819820)

### Inferences
- Heavy retouching (skin smoothing, slimming) is still the default expectation. The appetite for natural/documentary and film looks opens a gap for a lighter-retouch, story-driven product.
- Video (婚礼MV, short vertical clips for Douyin/Xiaohongshu) is often an add-on. Drone usually costs extra (+~US$30 in the Airbnb example).

### Gaps
- No reliable data on how often video is added or what it costs, or on Douyin/Xiaohongshu format preferences.
- I could not verify the volume of Xiaohongshu posts or which hashtags are trending.

## 4. Bali 旅拍 prices (RMB), inclusions, sellers

### Takeaway
Chinese-run Bali studios charge about ¥7k–15k for basic packages. Branded "all-inclusive" packages start around ¥20k–26k, and destination weddings from about ¥33k. Cliff wedding venues cost ¥80–150k. Total couple spend, including flights and hotel, is about ¥20–30k+.

### Cited Findings
- Starting prices quoted in 2026 guides: 芙拉薇尔 Bali ¥19,999+ (airport pickup, visa help, sea-view honeymoon hotel, island-wide car). 巴厘印象 ¥20,999+ (venues, car and hotel all-in). 铂爵旅拍 ¥25,999+. 巴厘秘境 (Kuta) ¥6,899–14,999. Pre-wedding photos from ¥19,999, destination wedding from ¥32,999. A high-end package of ¥19,999 covers 1 day with 4 outfits each. — [Sina guide](https://k.sina.com.cn/article_7906341801_1d74133a900102205w.html); [Sina ranking](https://k.sina.cn/article_7879922979_1d5ae152301901ph7q.html?from=travel); [qdxw](https://wap.qdxw.com.cn/content/20588/88/69249.html) (promotional; "起" means a base price)
- Older packages at ¥10,999–11,999: 3–6 outfits each, 40–50 精修, airport pickup and hotel included. — [Zhihu older](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/37758948)
- Wedding-site data: Bali packages run about ¥7k–15k, with a total budget including flights and hotel of about ¥20–30k. — [hunliji](https://www.hunliji.com/bai_ke/detail_9002); [91hunbohui](https://www.91hunbohui.com/post/323.html)
- Tirtha Bridal (Uluwatu cliff chapel) runs about ¥80–150k per wedding (venue, décor, catering). — [travelarbitrage 2026](https://www.travelarbitrage.net/blog/bali-wedding-venues-2026)
- Sellers: Chinese chain 旅拍 brands with Bali branches (铂爵 and others), Bali-based Chinese studios, Beijing wedding planners selling Bali/Phuket/Okinawa weddings (e.g. 爱薇时), OTAs (Tuniu historically; toursforfun CN listings), and Airbnb experiences. — [iwishwed](http://www.iwishwed.net/); [toursforfun](https://cn.toursforfun.com/default/nid-12942.html); [China Daily 2018](https://www.chinadaily.com.cn/a/201806/16/WS5b24503aa310010f8f59d4ab.html)

### Inferences
- For a local Bali studio, a credible entry point is about ¥6–10k (US$850–1,400): 1 day, MUA/hair, 3–4 outfits each, 40–50 精修, all raw files, car. That undercuts the branded ¥20k packages, which bundle the hotel.
- Common upsells: extra outfits, extra 精修 at a per-photo price, drone, MV/short video, a second location day, albums.

### Gaps
- No Trip.com/Dianping listing prices verified. No verified reviews.

## 5. What makes them pay a premium

### Takeaway
They pay more for trust: a Chinese-speaking team, makeup and dresses included, sample sets and reviews on Xiaohongshu/Dianping, an all-inclusive itinerary, and clear after-sales terms with no hidden fees.

### Cited Findings
- Guide titles stress 售后 (after-sales), 避坑 (avoid traps) and 口碑 (reputation), and pitch "one price, all-in" and pickup/visa help as reasons to choose. — [Sina](https://k.sina.com.cn/article_7906341801_1d74133a900102205w.html); [qdxw](https://wap.qdxw.com.cn/content/20588/88/69249.html)
- Warnings: low-price packages often have only 30–40 精修 and leave out tickets, transport and hotel. Extra 精修 cost more, so contract terms matter. — [Weibo 2026](https://weibo.com/2/detail/comos:niuqcsv3330096); [hunliji](https://www.hunliji.com/bai_ke/detail_44214)
- Maldives shows that permission and access rules (local photographers only) raise prices. — [Zhihu](https://www.zhihu.com/question/41467065/answer/214181507)

### Inferences
- A Bali studio targeting Chinese clients needs: a Chinese-language (WeChat/Xiaohongshu) presence, Mandarin coordination, transparent per-item pricing, sample albums in trending styles (film, Korean, documentary), and fast 精修 delivery (≤30 days beats the norm).

### Gaps
- No survey data quantifying willingness to pay for a Mandarin-speaking team or for brand.
