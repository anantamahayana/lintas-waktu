# Selling Bali photo/video shoots to mainland Chinese clients: channels, intermediaries, payment, language, competition

Research date: 2026-10-11. Nine web searches, no full-page fetches. Snippets come through a search summarizer, so check key numbers at the source before using them. Labels used: **[Evidence]** = stated by a cited source; **[Opinion]** = source's view or marketing claim. Everything under "Inferences" is the researcher's own reasoning.

## Market size context

### Takeaway
China is a large and fast-growing source market for Bali again: about 537,000 arrivals in 2025, and February 2026 arrivals almost doubled year on year.

### Cited Findings
- [Evidence] Bali international arrivals were 6.95M in 2025 (+10%). China was up 20% to about 537,000 (537,380, 7.73% share), behind Australia (1.63M) and India (569,260). — [Skift DLR](https://dlr.skift.com/?p=5716); [NomadLawyer](https://nomadlawyer.org/bali-tourism-market-trends-2026)
- [Evidence] In 2024, 448,000 Chinese visited Bali, ranking 4th. — [China Daily HK](https://chinadailyhk.com/article/607178)
- [Evidence] BPS Bali: February 2026 growth was driven mainly by China, up 98.54% compared with February 2025. — [BPS Bali](https://bali.bps.go.id/en/news/2026/04/01/354/kunjungan-wisman-ke-bali-pada-februari-2026-meningkat.html)
- [Evidence] Indonesia-wide Chinese arrivals were reported at more than 1.34M in 2025, a six-year high. — [The Asian Banker](https://www.theasianbanker.com/press-releases/china-indonesia-qr-payment-link-connects-40-million-merchants-to-chinese-travellers)

### Inferences
- The demand is real and growing. February is around Chinese New Year, so part of that spike may be seasonal.

### Gaps
- I found no data on what share of Chinese visitors book paid photo shoots.

## Platforms (RED, Douyin, Dianping, Trip.com/Ctrip, Fliggy, Mafengwo, WeChat): can an Indonesian business list?

### Takeaway
Trip.com/Ctrip has an open supplier onboarding route for overseas tours and activities. This is the most realistic formal listing. A Xiaohongshu store needs a brand-type store, a deposit and (for some features) a Chinese ID. Organic posting on RED/WeChat, or an intermediary, is the practical route for a small duo.

### Cited Findings
- [Evidence] Trip.com "Attractions & Tours" supplier onboarding has 4 steps: apply (~1 hour), review (2–3 working days), contract (~1 day), account setup. Products are distributed on Trip.com and Ctrip apps plus B2B partners, in 140+ countries. — [Trip.com Join Platform](https://sa.trip.com/m/vbooking/home)
- [Evidence] Activity aggregators such as Hotelbeds feed their catalogue into Trip.com by API, which is an indirect route. — [TravelDailyNews](https://www.traveldailynews.com/hotels-lodging/hotelbeds-signs-ancillary-distribution-agreement-with-trip-com-group/)
- [Evidence] Supplier commission rates for Trip.com activities were not public in the sources found.
- [Evidence, vendor blogs] On Xiaohongshu, overseas companies must open a specialist, authorised or flagship store with company and brand documents. The zero-cost trial is for individual stores only. Hong Kong example: RMB 600 review fee, deposit from RMB 20,000, 0.7% payment fee. — [Wise](https://wise.com/en-cn/blog/red-note-opening-store-guide); [Airwallex](https://www.airwallex.com/en-hk/blog/xiaohongshu-store-hong-kong-guide)
- [Evidence] Running online stores and livestreaming on RedNote still require a Chinese ID. — [Rest of World 2025](https://restofworld.org/2025/xiaohongshu-rednote-expands-overseas-tiktok-refugees/)
- [Evidence] Airbnb (Chinese interface zh.airbnb.com) lists many Bali photographer "services": couple shoots of 2 hours for about $93, family shoots of 1–2 hours for $95–178, Ubud portraits for $80–132, 12-hour day tours for $462. — [Airbnb example](https://zh.airbnb.com/services/6829251); [Airbnb](https://zh.airbnb.com/services/6806550)

### Inferences
- Path of least resistance: (1) a Trip.com supplier application for a fixed "Bali travel photo" product; (2) organic Xiaohongshu notes in Chinese, with sample galleries and location guides, leading to WeChat contact; (3) Airbnb Services, which reaches some Chinese users.
- Dianping, Douyin local services, Fliggy and Mafengwo were not verified. They usually require Chinese entities or local agents, so treat them as unconfirmed.

### Gaps
- Official requirements and commissions for Dianping overseas, Douyin 团购 overseas, Fliggy and Mafengwo. No reliable sources were found.
- Trip.com activity commission percentage. You need to ask the supplier team.
- Whether RED blocks or limits notes that include WeChat IDs or external contacts (often reported anecdotally, not verified here).

## Intermediaries (Chinese 旅拍 agencies, Chinese-run tour agencies, hotels)

### Takeaway
I found no sourced figures on what Chinese 旅拍 agencies pay Bali subcontractors. The Chinese tour ecosystem in Bali has a history of "zero-dollar" tours and unlicensed Chinese guides acting as photographers, so choose partners carefully.

### Cited Findings
- [Evidence, 2018–2019] "Zero dollar" tours sent Chinese tourists to Chinese-owned shops, with payment often by WeChat Pay outside Indonesia. Up to 3 in 10 Chinese tourists were in this category. Authorities closed shops and listed good and bad operators. — [Caixin 2019](https://www.caixinglobal.com/2019-12-06/bali-cracks-down-on-dirt-cheap-package-tours-from-china-101491387.html); [Canberra Times 2018](https://www.canberratimes.com.au/world/asia/china-s-tourism-boom-prompts-fears-that-bali-is-being-sold-cheap-20180813-p4zx5m.html)
- [Evidence, 2018] A Bali legislator reported Chinese tour guides working as photographers and urged a crackdown. Guides need a provincial license. — [Republika](https://en.republika.co.id/berita/en/national-politics/p932fu414/bali-satpol-pp-to-crack-down-on-foreign-tourist-guides)

### Inferences
- Legally, an Indonesian duo is in a good position to be the compliant local subcontractor for Chinese agencies and guides, because foreign photographers face deportation risk (see Competition). That legal standing can be part of the pitch.
- Avoid deals where the client pays the agency in China and the duo is paid late or at a very low rate. Ask for a deposit before the shoot.

### Gaps
- Typical subcontract rates and agency markups for Bali 旅拍. You would need to get this from interviews or from WeChat quotes.
- Which hotels and villas are popular with Chinese guests. Not found.

## Payment and communication

### Takeaway
Since 2025–2026, Chinese Alipay (and UnionPay) users can pay any Indonesian QRIS merchant directly, so a duo with a normal QRIS code can take Chinese payments with no new hardware. WeChat Pay was not confirmed live as of mid-2026.

### Cited Findings
- [Evidence] Bank Indonesia–PBoC pilot from 17 Aug 2025: 1.64M inbound transactions, Rp556B (~US$32.1M). Soft launch 30 Apr 2026 in Beijing. — [Antara](https://en.antaranews.com/news/414208); [China Global South](https://chinaglobalsouth.com/2025/10/03/china-connects-alipay-wechat-indonesia-qr-code/)
- [Evidence] The link runs via Alipay+ (Ant International) and UnionPay International. Alipay and UnionPay users can pay at more than 40M QRIS merchants, and an existing merchant QRIS code is enough. — [The Asian Banker](https://www.theasianbanker.com/press-releases/china-indonesia-qr-payment-link-connects-40-million-merchants-to-chinese-travellers); [finews.asia](https://www.finews.asia/finance/44874-indonesia-china-qr-payments-alipay-union-pay)
- [Evidence; sources conflict on scope] Antara says only Alipay is live. Gotrade says Alipay and UnionPay are live and WeChat Pay is in technical review. RRI says a grand launch was planned for June 2026 in Shanghai. — [Gotrade](https://www.heygotrade.com/en/news/indonesia-launches-cross-border-qris-with-china-today/); [RRI](https://rri.co.id/en/business/2380635/qris-cross-border-payments-bring-indonesia-china-tourism-closer)

### Inferences
- Practical setup: take deposits through QRIS, which Alipay users can scan, and offer a Wise or PayPal link as a fallback. Check WeChat Pay status before promising it.
- Communication (not sourced here): Chinese clients normally expect WeChat contact and fast replies, and Google services are blocked in mainland China, so Google Drive delivery will fail without a VPN. These are widely known facts but have no citation in these notes.

### Gaps
- No sources were gathered on response-time expectations, contract norms, or how Baidu Netdisk compares with WeTransfer, Pixieset or Alibaba Cloud for delivery.

## Competitors (Chinese studios and freelancers in Bali)

### Takeaway
Chinese and other foreign photographers work in Bali, often on tourist visas. Indonesia has been deporting them, which pushes demand toward legal local providers. Local price benchmarks on Airbnb are about $80–200 for 1–2 hours.

### Cited Findings
- [Evidence, 2026] Indonesia deported 25 foreigners running illegal photography businesses in Medan, Jakarta, Bali and Surabaya, mostly Chinese and European, after complaints from local photographers. — [Antara](https://en.antaranews.com/news/418561/indonesia-deports-25-foreigners-over-illegal-photography-work)
- [Evidence/report, Jun 2026] Bali immigration is targeting photographers and creators on tourist visas, reportedly including unpaid or portfolio shoots. — [PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2026/06/12/bali-is-cracking-down-on-photographers-traveling-with-tourist-visas/)
- [Evidence] Local price samples: see the Airbnb figures above. Bali Image Photography charges a 50% fee for cancellations within 30 days. — [Bali Image Photo](https://baliimagephoto.mypixieset.com/bali-wedding-photography-package/)

### Inferences
- Positioning: "licensed local team, Chinese-language service, Alipay-ready, fast delivery".

### Gaps
- Pricing of specific Chinese-run Bali studios. These are mostly visible only on RED/Dianping/WeChat, and searching did not surface them.

## Cultural expectations and pitfalls

### Takeaway
No sources were gathered in this pass. Everything below is unsourced general knowledge and needs to be verified.

### Cited Findings
- None.

### Inferences
- [Opinion, unsourced] Chinese clients expect heavy retouching (skin, slimming), a 精修 (fine-retouch) count stated in the package, red and gold as positive colours, white or black mourning connotations in some contexts, and auspicious wedding dates. Ask permission before posting faces publicly, and deliver through a link that works in China.

### Gaps
- Needs sourced Chinese-language material on 旅拍 retouching norms and privacy expectations.

## Other Asian markets as easier entry

### Takeaway
There is weak evidence only. Pre-wedding photo culture comes from Taiwan and spread to China and Korea, but I found no Bali-specific data for Korean or Taiwanese clients.

### Cited Findings
- [Evidence] Engagement and pre-wedding photo culture started in Taiwan and spread to China and South Korea. — [KDVR/AP feature](https://digital-release.kdvr.com/?p=206338)
- [Opinion, marketing] A Korean-style studio markets Bali pre-wedding shoots. — [OneThreeOneFour](https://www.onethreeonefour.com/blog/tag/pre-wedding)

### Inferences
- Malaysia, Singapore and Hong Kong clients use WhatsApp, Instagram and Google, so they need no platform or payment workarounds. They are the low-friction way to test Chinese-language marketing.

### Gaps
- Arrival numbers and photo demand for Korea, Taiwan, Hong Kong and Malaysia in Bali were not researched.
