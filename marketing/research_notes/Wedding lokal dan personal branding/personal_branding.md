## Who is Faim Achmad: work, style, platforms, monetisation

### Takeaway
Faim Achmad is a Cirebon-based **videographer** (not primarily a photographer) and educator. He runs the "SATSETSHOOT" brand ("sat-set" = quick and simple videography), is linked to Fujifilm (reported as a Fujifilm X-Team member), and earns from workshops, talks and brand partnerships (Godox, Hollyland, Fujifilm), with a specialty in **personal profile videos** shot with gimbal movement.

## Who buys personal-branding shoots and what they want

### Takeaway
Bali sellers target wellness, yoga and fitness coaches, founders and creators, and visiting expats or nomads. Deliverables are bundles of edited photos across outfits and locations, increasingly with a short video or reel.

### Cited Findings
- Bali Image Photography publishes personal-branding case pages for yoga, wellness, fitness (waterfall + gym), resort solo branding (Tanah Gajah, Ubud), Soulshine Bali, and sunset at Seseh Beach — [overview](https://baliimagephoto.mypixieset.com/blog/personal-branding-photoshoot-bali/); [yoga](https://baliimagephoto.mypixieset.com/blog/yoga-personal-branding-photography-bali/); [wellness](https://baliimagephoto.mypixieset.com/blog/wellness-personal-branding-photography-bali/); [waterfall/gym](https://baliimagephoto.mypixieset.com/blog/personal-branding-photoshoot-bali-waterfall-gym/)

### Takeaway

## Minimal gear & BTS

### Takeaway
I found no sourced gear lists for this market. Faim's brand ties (Fujifilm body, Godox lighting, Hollyland wireless audio, gimbal) suggest a compact kit.

### Cited Findings
- Faim's sponsors and partners include Godox (lighting) and Hollyland (wireless mics) — [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/faim_achmad/); his gimbal workshop with a model — [Fujishop](https://fujishop.id/event/smooth-and-creattive-videeo-movement-with-faim-achmad/); his course stresses gear choice, colour, light and audio — [satsetshoot.com](https://satsetshoot.com/)

### Inferences
- Opinion, practitioner consensus, not sourced here:
  - Lenses: an 85mm (or 50mm) prime for headshots and a 35mm for lifestyle.
  - Light: a 5-in-1 reflector, with open shade or golden hour in Bali.
  - One Godox speedlight or small LED bounced or through a softbox.
  - Audio and stabilisation: a wireless lavalier for talking-head reels; a phone or small gimbal for BTS.
  - Workflow: film the second shooter's BTS on a phone during every shoot, then post a before/after or raw-vs-edit reel.

- No evidence quantifying how many bookings BTS content drives.
